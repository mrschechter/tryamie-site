Important Safety Information

Who should not use Naltrexone

  • People currently using opioids or opioid-containing medications
  • Those in acute opioid withdrawal
  • Anyone with acute hepatitis or liver failure
  • People who have had an allergic reaction to naltrexone

Common side effects

May include nausea, headache, dizziness, fatigue, insomnia, and anxiety. These typically resolve within the first 1-2 weeks of treatment.

Important warnings

  • Do not take opioid medications while on Naltrexone—it can cause sudden opioid withdrawal
  • Tell your doctor about all medications you take, including over-the-counter medicines
  • Naltrexone may cause liver damage in high doses—your doctor may monitor liver function
  • Carry a medical alert card indicating you take Naltrexone

This is not a complete list of safety information. View full prescribing information or talk to your Amie provider.
