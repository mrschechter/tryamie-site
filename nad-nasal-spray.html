Important Safety Information

What is NAD+ Nasal Spray?

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) nasal spray is a prescription formulation that delivers NAD+ directly to the nasal mucosa for rapid absorption into the bloodstream.

Who should not use NAD+ Nasal Spray?

Do not use if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. Consult your doctor before use if you have any chronic nasal conditions, are taking any medications, or have a history of allergies to NAD+ or related compounds.

Common side effects

Side effects may include:

  • Mild nasal irritation or dryness
  • Temporary flushing or warmth
  • Mild headache (usually resolves within first week)
  • Increased energy that may affect sleep if taken late in day

How to use

Use as directed by your physician. Typically 1-2 sprays per nostril, once or twice daily. Best taken in the morning or early afternoon to avoid affecting sleep.
