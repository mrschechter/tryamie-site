Important to Know

The Little Pink Pill contains PT-141 (Bremelanotide), a prescription medication that works by activating melanocortin receptors in your brain. Unlike other treatments that focus on blood flow, PT-141 targets the neurological pathways that control sexual desire and arousal.
PT-141 is administered as a subcutaneous injection approximately 45 minutes before anticipated sexual activity. Each dose comes pre-measured for your convenience. Most women find the process simple after the first use. We provide detailed instructions and support.
Common side effects may include flushing, nausea, and headache. These are typically mild and temporary. Our physicians will review your health history to ensure PT-141 is appropriate for you and discuss any potential risks.
PT-141 is not recommended for women with uncontrolled high blood pressure, cardiovascular disease, or those who are pregnant or breastfeeding. Our physicians will conduct a thorough review of your health history during the consultation process.
