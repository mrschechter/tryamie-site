Semaglutide vs Tirzepatide: Which GLP-1 Is Right for You?

📅 January 2, 2026 ⏱️ 10 min read ✍️ Try Amie Medical Team

Two medications have dominated the weight loss conversation: semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound). Both are highly effective GLP-1 medications—but they work differently and produce different results.

If you're deciding between the two, this comprehensive comparison will help you understand the key differences and determine which might be better for your goals.

The Quick Answer

Bottom Line

Tirzepatide shows higher average weight loss (up to 22.5% in trials vs. 15% for semaglutide). However, semaglutide has a longer track record, more real-world data, and is often more accessible. Both are excellent options—the "best" choice depends on your specific situation, insurance, and provider recommendations.

How They Work: The Key Difference

Semaglutide: Single-Target GLP-1

Semaglutide mimics a single hormone: GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1). This hormone:

  • Reduces appetite by acting on brain hunger centers
  • Slows gastric emptying so you feel full longer
  • Improves blood sugar control

Tirzepatide: Dual-Target GLP-1/GIP

Tirzepatide targets two hormones: GLP-1 and GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide). This dual mechanism:

  • Provides all the benefits of GLP-1
  • GIP adds additional metabolic effects on fat tissue
  • May provide greater satiety and metabolic improvements

Think of it like this: semaglutide is a powerful single-action tool, while tirzepatide attacks weight from two angles simultaneously.

Weight Loss Results: The Data

Semaglutide (STEP Trials)

The STEP 1 trial followed 1,961 adults over 68 weeks:

  • 14.9% average weight loss (vs. 2.4% placebo)
  • 32% of participants lost 20%+ of body weight
  • 86% lost at least 5% of body weight

Tirzepatide (SURMOUNT Trials)

The SURMOUNT-1 trial followed 2,539 adults over 72 weeks:

  • Up to 22.5% average weight loss at highest dose
  • 36% of participants lost 25%+ of body weight
  • Over 90% lost at least 5% of body weight
Real-World Context

For a 200 lb person: semaglutide would mean losing ~30 lbs on average, while tirzepatide would mean losing ~40-45 lbs. That's a significant difference for many people.

Side Effects Compared

Both medications share similar GLP-1 side effects, primarily gastrointestinal:

  • Nausea: Common with both; tirzepatide may cause slightly less
  • Diarrhea/Constipation: Similar rates
  • Vomiting: Similar rates, typically mild
  • Fatigue: More commonly reported with tirzepatide

Most side effects are temporary and improve as your body adjusts to the medication. The gradual dose escalation with both medications helps minimize discomfort.

Cost Comparison

Without insurance, both brand-name medications are expensive ($1,000-1,500/month). However:

  • Compounded semaglutide is more widely available and affordable (starting around $147/month at Try Amie)
  • Compounded tirzepatide availability is more limited due to supply constraints
  • Insurance coverage varies widely for both medications

Which Should You Choose?

Consider Semaglutide If:

  • You want a medication with a longer track record
  • Cost is a major factor (compounded options more available)
  • You're starting with weight loss medications for the first time
  • Your doctor recommends starting with a single-mechanism approach

Consider Tirzepatide If:

  • Maximum weight loss is your primary goal
  • You have significant insulin resistance or type 2 diabetes
  • You've tried semaglutide and want more aggressive results
  • You have access through insurance or can afford brand-name pricing

What About Switching?

Some patients start with semaglutide and later switch to tirzepatide for enhanced results. This is a legitimate strategy, but should be done under medical supervision. Factors to consider:

  • You may experience renewed GI side effects when switching
  • Dosing doesn't directly convert—your doctor will determine appropriate starting dose
  • Insurance may require stepping through one medication before approving another

The Verdict

Both semaglutide and tirzepatide are excellent, science-backed weight loss medications. Tirzepatide shows superior weight loss results in clinical trials, but semaglutide remains a highly effective option with better accessibility and more real-world data.

The best choice depends on your individual circumstances—health history, goals, budget, and what your healthcare provider recommends. Many patients do extremely well on either medication.
